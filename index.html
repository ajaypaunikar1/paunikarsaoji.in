About Us

Paunikar Saoji brings the authentic, fiery flavours of Maharashtra's Saoji cuisine to Besa. Every dish is crafted with a rustic blend of spices, slow-cooked to perfection — just like the traditional recipes passed down through generations.

We believe in bold taste, quality ingredients, and the warmth of a community kitchen. From tender mutton handis to flavourful vegetarian delights, our menu is a tribute to the rich culinary heritage of Vidarbha.
